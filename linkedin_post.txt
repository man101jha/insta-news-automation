🚀 Automating My Daily Financial Instagram Carousels Using AI Agents! 📈

I wanted a way to curate, write, design, review, and post daily Indian financial news to Instagram on autopilot. So, I built a multi-agent system from scratch using Python, Groq LLM, and Playwright, and scheduled it on GitHub Actions!

Here is how the automated workflow behaves every morning at 8:40 AM IST:

🔍 1. Scraping: A News Scraper collects recent headlines from Economic Times Markets, Livemint, Moneycontrol, and r/IndiaInvestments.
🧠 2. AI Curation: A Groq LLM agent (llama-3.3-70b-versatile) filters the top 5 most actionable stories for retail investors.
✍️ 3. Copywriting: An LLM copywriter agent structures a 7-slide deck copy (detailed 7-8 lines paragraph summaries with metric tags).
🎨 4. Design & Render: Playwright parses HTML/CSS to screenshot 1080x1080px dark-slate slides. It dynamically scrapes the original article images (using og:image tags) for background overlays.
💬 5. Human-in-the-Loop: Previews are sent to a private Telegram Group. Any admin can click "Approve" or "Reject" with live user attribution (e.g. "Approved by Mangesh").
📸 6. Publish: Once approved, the poster posts the carousel to Instagram via the Graph API and purges local temp images.
